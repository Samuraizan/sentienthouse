# Event Recap

## Triggers
- "event recap"
- "post-event report"
- "how did the event go"

## Purpose
After every Zo House event, compile a structured post-event report and generate social media recap content. The recap serves two purposes: internal learning (what worked, what didn't) and external storytelling (showing the world what Zo is about).

## Timeline
- **Target:** Recap completed within 1 week of event
- **Social recap posts:** Within 48 hours while momentum is fresh
- **Internal report:** Within 5 days for thorough data collection

## Data Collection

### Registration & Attendance (from luma-sync)
- Total registered
- Total checked in (attended)
- Show-up rate (%)
- Waitlist count (if any)
- Walk-ins (from door count, ask Boldrin or house captain)

### Revenue (from rev-tracking)
- Ticket revenue (if paid event)
- Sponsorship revenue (if applicable)
- F&B revenue
- Total revenue
- Total costs
- Net profit/loss

### Qualitative Data
- Host satisfaction: Did the host/speaker enjoy it? Would they do it again? (Get this via DM or quick call)
- Attendee feedback: Any notable comments, DMs, tweets about the event
- Vibe check: Was the energy right? Too crowded? Too sparse? Good conversations happening?
- Photos/videos: Collect from house photographer, attendees (with permission), Instagram tags

## Social Media Recap

### LinkedIn Recap Post
- **Format:** Storytelling post with key numbers woven in
- **Angle:** "Here's what happened when..." or "X people showed up to..."
- **Include:** 1-2 standout numbers, a human moment, what it says about the Zo community
- **Photos:** Carousel of 3-5 best photos
- **Tag:** Host, speakers, sponsors, notable attendees (with permission)

### X / Twitter Recap
- **Format:** Thread of 3-5 tweets
- **Tweet 1:** Hook + best photo
- **Tweet 2-3:** Highlights and moments
- **Tweet 4:** Numbers (if impressive)
- **Final tweet:** "Next one is on [date]" or "Follow @blrxzo for the next one"

### Instagram Recap
- **Format:** Carousel post (8-10 slides) or Reel
- **Slide 1:** Event title + hero shot
- **Slides 2-7:** Best moments, candid shots, speaker on stage, crowd shots
- **Slide 8:** Key stats in designed format
- **Final slide:** "See you at the next one" + follow CTA
- **Caption:** Brief story of the night, tag people, Luma link for next event

### Farcaster
- Short recap cast with 1 photo and a vibe summary
- Link to longer recap if published on blog/mirror

## Photo Guidelines
- Only use photos where attendees are clearly visible with their implicit or explicit consent (public event, visible camera)
- Prioritize: speaker/host shots, crowd energy shots, candid conversations, the venue looking alive
- Avoid: unflattering angles, people clearly not wanting to be photographed, empty room shots
- Credit the photographer always

## Important Notes
- The recap is content gold -- treat it that way. A good recap post can drive more signups than the original promo.
- Always check with Boldrin before posting numbers publicly (especially revenue)
- If the event underperformed, still do the internal recap honestly -- that's how we improve. The social recap can just focus on the highlights.
- Cross-post the internal report to the events channel for the whole team to see
- Archive photos in a shared folder organized by event date and name
